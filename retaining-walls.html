Retaining Wall Contractor in Cheyenne, Wyoming

When a slope needs to be tamed or a yard needs another level of usable space, a well-built retaining wall does the work — and looks good doing it. Q Lawn Landscaping designs and installs block and natural-stone retaining walls throughout Cheyenne and the surrounding Wyoming communities, built to handle our freeze-thaw winters.

We build straight runs, sweeping curves, terraced multi-level walls, and boulder walls — with proper drainage, base preparation, and compaction behind every one, because that's what keeps a wall standing long after the first hard winter. Whether you're holding back a hillside, creating a raised planting bed, or leveling a backyard for a patio, we handle the full job from grading to the final capstone.

Our crews have built retaining walls across Cheyenne for over a decade, for both homeowners and commercial properties. If another contractor told you the job was too steep or too tricky, that's usually the kind of wall we like to build.

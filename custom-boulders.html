Custom & Decorative Boulders in Cheyenne, Wyoming

We're exploring a new custom boulder offering for Cheyenne-area homes and businesses. Concept examples shown below. This is a preview, not an active service: nothing on this page is available to order today, and no specific stone, size or design is guaranteed.

Natural stone has always been part of how we build a Wyoming yard — a single boulder anchoring a rock bed, a grouping that breaks up a long run of river rock, a stone that makes a front entry feel finished. We're now planning to offer that stone as its own line for Cheyenne: custom address boulders with your house number, engraved family-name and ranch boulders, business logo stones, and decorative landscape boulders chosen for this climate and set by the Q Lawn crew.

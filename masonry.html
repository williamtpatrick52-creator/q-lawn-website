Masonry & Hardscaping in Cheyenne, Wyoming

Great outdoor spaces are built on solid stonework. Q Lawn Landscaping provides landscape masonry and hardscaping across Cheyenne, Wyoming — the block, paver, and natural-stone features that turn a plain yard into a place you actually want to spend time.

Our hardscape masonry includes seat walls, fire pits, stone steps, columns and pillars, paver patios and walkways, and natural-stone accents. Every piece is built on a properly prepared base and set to handle Wyoming's freeze-thaw cycles, so your fire pit and seat wall look as good in year ten as they did on day one.

This is outdoor, landscape-focused masonry — the stonework that ties a patio, wall, and fire feature into one cohesive backyard. We'll help you choose materials and colors that fit your home and the Cheyenne climate, then build it to last. From a single fire pit to a full outdoor-living build with patios, seat walls, and steps, Q Lawn brings over a decade of local craftsmanship to every project.
